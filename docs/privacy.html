CoRiderly Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

Terms and Conditions

CoRiderly ("we", "our", "the app", package com.coriderly.app) is a carpooling app that helps people find and offer rides. This policy explains what data the app collects, how it is used and shared, and your choices.

1. Information we collect

  • Account details: your name, mobile number and an MPIN you choose. Your email address is optional.
  • Ride details: rides you offer (origin, destination, date, time, seats, price, vehicle information and the contact phone number you enter).
  • Bookings and reviews: rides you book or cancel, and the ratings and feedback you write about drivers.

We do not collect your precise device location, contacts, photos, or advertising ID, and the app contains no advertising or third-party analytics SDKs.

2. How we use information

  • To create and secure your account and sign you in.
  • To show, offer, book and cancel rides.
  • To let riders contact drivers about a ride.
  • To show driver ratings and feedback to other users.
  • To maintain the safety, reliability and legal compliance of the service.

3. Sharing

  • Other users: ride details, the driver's contact number and name, and reviews are visible to other signed-in users so rides can be arranged.
  • Service provider: data is stored and processed on our backend provider, Supabase, which hosts the database and authentication on our behalf.
  • We do not sell your personal data. We may disclose data if required by law.

4. Security

Data is transmitted over HTTPS and access to stored records is restricted with row-level security rules. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

5. Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us at the address below; we will act on requests within a reasonable period.

6. Children

CoRiderly is not intended for children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version will always be available at this page, with the date above updated.
