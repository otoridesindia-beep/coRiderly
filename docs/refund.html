CoRiderly Refund Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

This Refund Policy explains when the ride publishing fee paid on CoRiderly can be refunded. It applies only to the platform fee paid to CoRiderly (currently ₹39 per ride), not to any amount a rider and driver agree between themselves for a trip.

1. Refund amount

When a refund is approved, 50% of the publishing fee is refunded. For the current fee of ₹39, the refund is ₹19.50. The remaining 50% covers payment processing and platform costs and is not refundable.

2. When a refund may be approved

  • You cancel a published ride before any rider has booked a seat on it.
  • Your payment was successful but the ride could not be published because of a technical problem on our side.
  • The ride was removed by CoRiderly for a reason that was not caused by you breaking our Terms and Conditions.

3. When a refund is not available

  • A rider has already booked a seat on the ride.
  • The ride date has passed.
  • The ride was removed or your account was suspended for violating our Terms and Conditions.
  • The ride was cancelled or changed by you after riders booked it, or a trip was not completed for reasons between the driver and riders.

4. Duplicate or failed payments

If you were charged more than once for the same ride, or money was debited but the payment failed and no ride was published, contact us. Where the duplicate or failed charge is confirmed, the extra or failed amount is refunded in full.

5. How to request a refund

Send your request to the address below with your registered mobile number, the ride details (route and date), and the payment reference from Pine Labs. Please request within 7 days of the payment.

6. Processing time

Approved refunds are sent to the original payment method through our payment provider, Pine Labs. They normally reach your account within 5 to 7 working days after approval, depending on your bank or card issuer.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version will always be available on this page with the date above updated. See also our Terms and Conditions and Privacy Policy.
